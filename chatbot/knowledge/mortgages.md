# Mortgages (Home Loans)

## Standard Mortgage
- A long-term secured loan for buying residential property, with the property serving as
  collateral. Terms commonly range from 10 to 25-30 years.
- A down payment is usually required (often in the range of 10-20% of the property value),
  with the loan-to-value ratio affecting the offered interest rate.
- Interest can be fixed for the full term, fixed for an initial period then variable, or
  fully variable, depending on the specific mortgage product.

## Government / Subsidized Housing Programs
- Some markets offer government-backed or subsidized mortgage programs with reduced rates
  for first-time buyers, young families, or specific professions — eligibility rules and
  subsidy caps vary by program and change over time.

## Refinancing
- Existing mortgages (including ones from another bank) can often be refinanced to a new
  rate or term, which may involve appraisal and processing fees.

## Application Requirements (typical)
- Proof of income and employment, a property appraisal, property insurance, and a credit
  history check.
- Processing time from application to approval commonly takes from a few days to a few
  weeks depending on documentation completeness.
