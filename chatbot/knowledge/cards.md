## Debit Cards
- A standard debit card linked to a current account, usable for point-of-sale payments,
  ATM withdrawals, and online purchases.
- Comes with a companion mobile app for balance checks, transaction alerts, and card
  freeze/unfreeze.
- Typically free to open, sometimes with a modest monthly maintenance fee that may be
  waived above a minimum balance or with regular card usage.

## Credit Cards
- Revolving credit line with an interest-free grace period (commonly on the order of
  30-55 days) if the statement balance is paid in full each cycle.
- May include cashback or rewards points on everyday purchases, sometimes with bonus
  categories (groceries, fuel, online shopping).
- Requires an income and credit history check as part of the application; credit limit is
  set based on that assessment.

## Contactless & Mobile Payments
- Tap-to-pay (NFC) support and integration with mobile wallets is standard on modern
  debit and credit cards.
- Cards can usually be added to a phone's digital wallet directly from the bank's mobile
  app without waiting for the physical card to arrive.

## Card Security
- Instant card freeze/unfreeze from the mobile app if a card is lost or misplaced.
- Per-transaction and per-day spending limits can typically be set by the cardholder.
- Real-time push notifications for each transaction.
