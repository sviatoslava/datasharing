# Deposits & Savings

## Savings Account
- An account for setting money aside while keeping it accessible; interest usually accrues
  daily and is paid out monthly or quarterly.
- Withdrawals are typically unlimited or lightly limited (e.g. a small number of free
  withdrawals per month before a fee applies).
- No fixed term — funds can generally be added or withdrawn at any time.

## Term (Fixed) Deposits
- Money is locked in for a fixed term (common terms: 3, 6, 12, 24 months) in exchange for
  a higher interest rate than a regular savings account.
- Early withdrawal before the term ends usually reduces the interest earned, often down to
  the savings-account rate or lower.
- Interest can typically be paid monthly, at maturity, or capitalized (added back to the
  principal to compound).

## Currency Options
- Deposits are commonly offered in local currency as well as one or more foreign
  currencies (e.g. USD, EUR), with currency-specific rate tables.
- Foreign-currency deposits carry exchange-rate risk if converted back to local currency
  later.

## Opening a Deposit
- Can usually be opened either at a branch or through the mobile/online banking app.
- A minimum opening deposit amount is common, along with a minimum balance to keep the
  advertised rate.
