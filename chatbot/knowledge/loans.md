## Personal (Consumer) Loans
- Unsecured loans for general purposes (renovation, education, purchases, debt
  consolidation), usually repaid in fixed monthly installments over a set term (commonly
  6 months to 5-7 years).
- Interest rate and maximum amount depend on income, credit history, and existing debt
  load — an income and credit check is standard as part of underwriting.
- May allow early repayment, sometimes with an early-repayment fee depending on the terms
  agreed at signing.

## Auto Loans
- Secured loan for purchasing a vehicle, with the vehicle itself typically serving as
  collateral until the loan is repaid.
- Down payment requirements are common (e.g. a percentage of the vehicle price), reducing
  the loan-to-value ratio and often improving the offered rate.
- Term lengths are usually shorter than mortgages, commonly 1-7 years.

## Credit Line / Overdraft
- A revolving credit facility attached to a current account, letting the balance go
  temporarily negative up to an approved limit.
- Interest is charged only on the amount actually drawn, not the full approved limit.

## Application Requirements (typical)
- Proof of identity, proof of income (payslips, tax return, or bank statements), and a
  credit history check.
- Self-employed applicants are usually asked for additional income documentation.
