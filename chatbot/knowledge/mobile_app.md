# Mobile & Online Banking

> DEMO/PLACEHOLDER CONTENT — not real data from any specific bank. This file was written
> for a coding demo, modeled on typical retail banking products in general, not sourced
> from a live bank website. For actual current products, rates, and terms, check with your
> own bank directly.

## Core Features
- Balance and transaction history across all accounts and cards in one app.
- Peer-to-peer transfers (by phone number, card number, or account number), usually
  instant between accounts at the same bank.
- Bill payments and utility payments, often with saved payees for recurring bills.
- QR-code payments in stores, alongside standard tap-to-pay.

## Account Management
- Opening a new savings or deposit account entirely within the app, without a branch visit.
- Card controls: freeze/unfreeze, set spending limits, view card details, request a
  replacement.
- Loan and mortgage applications can often be started in-app, with document upload for
  supporting paperwork.

## Security
- Biometric login (fingerprint/face) alongside a PIN or password.
- Push notifications for every transaction.
- One-time codes (SMS or in-app) for confirming higher-risk actions like large transfers
  or adding a new payee.

## Support
- In-app chat support and a directory of nearby branches/ATMs with wait-time or status
  indicators are common features in modern banking apps.
